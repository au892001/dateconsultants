How we engage

Value in the first 90 days, not the second year

Every engagement produces something usable within six weeks. Long transformation programmes fail; sequenced increments that pay for the next one do not.

Diagnose

Two to three weeks of interviews, architecture review, and data profiling. You get a findings pack and a ranked opportunity list.

Prove

One narrow use case built end to end on your real data — enough to validate the architecture and the business case together.

Industrialise

Harden into a governed platform: CI/CD, testing, observability, cost controls, and documented runbooks.

Transfer

Paired delivery, enablement sessions, and an operating model your team owns. We plan our exit from day one.
